# FazWaz

> FazWaz is a Thailand-based property technology company operating one of the most comprehensive real estate portals in Southeast Asia. Its marketplace at fazwaz.com aggregates residential and investment property listings — condos, houses, villas, and land for sale and rent — connecting buyers, sellers, renters, and agents across Thailand and neighboring markets. FazWaz is backed by 500 Global.
